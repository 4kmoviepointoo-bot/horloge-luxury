Our Commitment

Sustainable Luxury

At HORLOGE, we believe that true luxury endures. Our commitment to sustainability is woven into every timepiece we create, from the sourcing of raw materials to the longevity of our finished products.

eco

Responsible Sourcing

We source precious metals and gemstones exclusively from certified ethical suppliers. Every diamond and sapphire is traceable to its origin, ensuring conflict-free procurement.

recycling

Circular Design

Each HORLOGE timepiece is designed for disassembly. Components can be replaced, serviced, and restored, extending the life of every watch for generations.

energy_savings_leaf

Carbon Neutral Operations

Our manufacture in Le Locle runs on 100% renewable energy. We offset all remaining emissions through verified carbon credits supporting reforestation projects.

water_drop

Water Conservation

Our closed-loop water system recycles 98% of water used in production. We continuously invest in technologies that reduce our environmental footprint.

"A watch made to last a century is the most sustainable product imaginable. It never becomes waste."

— Atelier founding principle, 1887

Looking Forward

By 2030, we aim to achieve full traceability across our entire supply chain, eliminate all single-use materials from our packaging, and transition to 100% recycled precious metals. Sustainability is not a destination but a journey we undertake with the same precision we apply to our craft.
