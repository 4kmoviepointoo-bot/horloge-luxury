Since 1887

A Legacy of
Excellence

For over a century, HORLOGE has defined the art of Swiss watchmaking. Our heritage is not merely a history — it is the foundation upon which every timepiece is crafted.

Our Story

Timeless Tradition

Founded in the Jura mountains of Switzerland, HORLOGE began as a small atelier dedicated to the pursuit of horological perfection. Each generation of master watchmakers has inherited not just techniques, but a philosophy: that a watch is not merely an instrument of time, but a companion for life.

1887

The Beginning

Master horologist Émile Horloge establishes his atelier in Vallée de Joux, creating pocket watches of extraordinary precision for European nobility.

1923

The First Wristwatch

HORLOGE pioneers the transition from pocket to wrist, unveiling the "Tempus" — a revolutionary timepiece that combined accuracy with wearable elegance.

1952

The Perpetual Movement

Introduction of the legendary Calibre 52, the world's thinnest perpetual calendar movement, establishing HORLOGE as a leader in mechanical innovation.

1989

The Artisan Collection

Launch of the Artisan line, featuring hand-engraved dials and gem-set bezels, merging haute horlogerie with fine jewelry craftsmanship.

Today

The Living Legacy

Five generations of expertise continue to define every HORLOGE timepiece, honoring tradition while embracing the future of watchmaking.

"A great watch does not merely tell time — it tells the story of its maker."

— Émile Horloge, Founder
